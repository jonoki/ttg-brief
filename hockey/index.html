What your scoring rewards

  • Goals and assists drive everything. There are no points for shots or faceoffs, so volume shooters lose value compared with Yahoo's default ranks.
  • Power-play points stack on top. A PP goal is worth 2.0 and a PP assist 1.5.
  • Hits and blocks at 0.1 each add up for defencemen. Seider, McAvoy and Samuelsson get 3–4 per game, which is worth about 25–30 points over a season.

Why defence is the edge

A replacement-level forward projects about 86 points, but a replacement defenceman only about 64. That makes a 95-point D worth more than a 110-point forward. Your Util slot can hold a D, so plan on four real defencemen. Byram moves up to 88 as Chicago's top-pair PP1 defenceman, which makes him a solid third or fourth D.

Goalies with weekly lineups

You start one goalie for the whole week, so starts count as much as quality. Wallstedt (24, projects 106) and Greaves (26, projects 103) are the best goalies left and still improving. Shesterkin, Oettinger, Swayman and Sorokin all project 99–102. Bobrovsky (38, new team) and Markstrom (37) project below replacement.

A loss costs −1 and each goal against costs −0.5, so a starter on a bad team (Saros, Knight, Askarov) scores less than his workload suggests.

Using the board

  • Mark every pick as it's made: ✕ for another team, Mine for yours. The pick counter moves forward on its own. Fix it with − and + if keeper slots throw it off.
  • A red edge and the won't last tag mean consensus ranks the player inside the picks left before your turn.
  • value means consensus ranks him 15 or more spots lower than your scoring does, so you can often wait on him. reach means the opposite.

How the projections work

  • Three seasons of scoring rate. Fantasy points per game in your scoring for 2025-26, 2024-25 and 2023-24, weighted 5 : 4 : 3 and by games played.
  • Regressed toward consensus. Each player gets 35 "ghost" games at the rate his consensus rank implies, so short samples don't swing wildly.
  • Age curve. Each season's rate is moved forward to his age on Jan 1, 2027. Rates rise 8–10% a year at 20–22 and 3–4% at 23–24, stay flat at 26–27, then fall 2–3% a year from 29, 4–5% from 32 and 7–8% from 35.

Roster and role changes

  • New teams and linemates. 120 offseason moves and camp line/PP reports were checked. Role changes move a player's rate up or down 5%, 10% or 20%, shown as ▲ or ▼ with the reason under his name.
  • Games played. Based on each player's last three seasons over an 84-game schedule, minus known absences: Jarvis 25, Bedard 15, Marchand 12, Terry 12, Fiala 11, McAvoy 6 (suspension).
  • Goalies. Three seasons of points per game, regressed toward an average starter, adjusted for age and new team, then multiplied by expected starts from the September depth charts.
